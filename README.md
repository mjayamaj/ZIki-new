# Ziki-new (Merged with Ziki Handshake)

A human-first redesign of the Ziki dashboard (**Zedu** workspace) merged with the **Ziki Handshake Protocol** to deliver calm, delightful, zero-trust human-AI engineering collaboration.

---

## What Was Merged from Ziki Handshake

1. **The 1-Tap Cryptographic Handshake Protocol**:
   - Zero-Trust guarantee: AI agents code, research, and test autonomously, but require an explicit 1-tap human handshake before any code can commit or merge.
   - Featured in **"Today's One Handshake"** with plain-English explanation, diff review, and collapsible *"Inspect why Gojo did this"* trust line.

2. **Blogger Copilot with Voice Synthesis**:
   - Blogger is integrated as the Lead AI Copilot alongside Gojo, Sukuna, and Six Eyes.
   - Built-in browser-native **Voice Engine**:
     - Voice memos and audio briefings playable directly with animated speech waveforms.
     - Conversational console where you can speak or type to Blogger, and Blogger answers aloud in clean synthesized voice.
     - Grounded in Google ADK (Agent Development Kit) deliberative ReAct planning.

3. **60s Fast Validation Offer Radar**:
   - Simulates and manages 60s fast PR review opportunities with live countdown timers.
   - One-tap accept (+10 pts) or pass, with voice briefings.

4. **Verified Handshake Ledger**:
   - Transparent, append-only audit trail logging every cryptographically signed handshake with timestamp, author, and credited bounty points.

5. **Safety Firewall (Standing Rules)**:
   - Interactive guardrails including Rule 1 (Zero-Trust Handshake required) and Rule 2 (Anti-collusion: Six Eyes cannot review Gojo PRs).
   - Ability to register new custom rules on the fly.

---

## Human-First Design Philosophy

- **Single Column (Max 960px)**: Focused, calm layout avoiding cognitive overload and metric bombardment.
- **Visual Distinction**: Circles for humans (`EM`), rounded-squares for AI teammates (`BL`, `GJ`, `SK`, `SE`).
- **Breathing Presence**: 2.4s calm pulse for online agents instead of static green lights.
- **Calm Mode**: One toggle hides all numbers, stats, and charts, showing only what requires human attention.
- **Forgiving Streaks & Appreciation**: Kind streak messaging that forgives missed weeks, plus a **"Say thanks ♡"** button that gives real gratitude and points.
